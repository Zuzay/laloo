The idea

We don't sell bathrooms.
We sell the walk.

Every walk to a bathroom passes shops, cafés and restaurants. Laloo lights up the right ones at the exact moment a traveler is looking at the map. Tourists, not locals: locals already know where to go.

For cities

Your public toilets, in five languages, for free.

We prepare a profile for each city from open data. The city claims it with an official email address, then marks toilets as closed, fixes hours and fees, and gets a “Verified by the city” badge. No fee, no contract, switch off any time.
